Applying Derivatives to Animal Husbandry Technology

Optimizing Beef Cattle Market Selection Using Instantaneous Growth Rates f'(t)

The Bioscience Scenario

In Malaysian commercial feedlots, optimizing production efficiency means identifying exactly when cattle reach their peak structural growth efficiency before fat deposition lowers feed conversion ratios. This application tracks a crossbred beef steer over a 48-month growth lifecycle.

By finding the derivative f'(t), we calculate the Average Daily Gain (ADG) expressed monthly. This metric tells production managers exactly when animal development shifts from rapid muscle accumulation to structural maintenance.

The Growth Function: Gompertz Model

f(t) = 450 * e^(-2.5 * e^(-0.08 * t))

Yields total animal weight in kilograms (kg) at month t.

The Derivative: Rate of Weight Gain

f'(t) = 0.08 * f(t) * ln(450 / f(t))

Yields the instantaneous growth velocity in kilograms per month (kg/month).
